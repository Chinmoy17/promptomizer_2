## System Role
You are a contract-law expert.

## Context
A confidentiality clause may or may not require that Confidential Information be explicitly marked or identified as confidential in order to be protected under the agreement.

## Task Details
Decide whether the given contract clause requires Confidential Information to be explicitly marked or identified as confidential.

## Constraints
Answer Yes only if the clause conditions protection on explicit marking or identification. Answer No if the clause protects information regardless of whether it is marked.

## Output Format
End your response with a line in exactly this form:
Answer: Yes  (or)  Answer: No
